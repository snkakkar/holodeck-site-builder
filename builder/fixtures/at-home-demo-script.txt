At Home Demo Script
Salesforce Customer Experience Vision

Customer: At Home Furniture (https://www.athome.com/)

Script Synopsis:
This demo showcases how At Home leverages Salesforce to create a personalized,
multi-visit customer journey for Rachel Morris, who is shopping for her annual
4th of July barbecue. It highlights Agentforce AI, MCP, and a connected
platform — from a personalized ad through a clienteling and service experience.

CX Summary:
An Instagram ad for a 4th of July sale leads to a personalized website
experience. A price drop alert via SMS brings the customer back to the site,
where she uses an AI Agent / MCP to find the perfect patio set and joins the
loyalty program. Proactive iMessage and email notifications confirm her order
and offer hosting tips. Later, she texts At Home to start an exchange and the
service agent handles it without a phone call.

Persona Description:
Rachel Morris is a 34-year-old homeowner who throws an annual 4th of July
barbecue. This year she is shopping for new outdoor furniture so the patio
feels perfect for friends and family. She is a busy parent who relies on
mobile, browses on her laptop in the evening, and expects retailers to
recognize her across channels.

Chapter 1 — Vision Setup
Section - Intro
Channel - presentation
Device - MacBook
1. Open the demo with the Salesforce Customer Experience Vision for At Home.
   Talk track: connected data + AI + commerce + service.
   Notes: keep the framing executive-friendly.

Chapter 2 — Discover
Section - Personalized advertising
Channel - Instagram
Device - iPhone
2. Rachel sees a personalized Instagram ad for At Home's Red, White & Blue
   Decor sale because Data Cloud identifies her as a high-propensity buyer for
   outdoor furniture.
   Notes: key data point for re-engagement; ad creative pulled from ad library.

Section - Personalized commerce landing
Channel - Website
Device - MacBook
3. She clicks to an At Home website experience built on Salesforce B2C
   Commerce. The hero banner and product recommendations are personalized
   to outdoor entertaining.
4. She browses patio sets and wishlists the Laguna Patio Set.
   Notes: wishlist becomes the re-engagement signal.

Chapter 3 — Re-engage
Section - Price drop and return
Channel - SMS
Device - iPhone
5. A few days later, a price drop on the Laguna Patio Set triggers a proactive
   SMS notification. Rachel taps the link and returns to the product page.

Chapter 4 — Decide with AI
Section - AI Design Assistant
Channel - Website chat
Device - MacBook
6. Rachel opens the At Home AI Design Assistant on the product page.
7. The agent answers questions about weather-resistant cushions and durability
   for a family with kids.
8. The agent recommends a matching umbrella and patriotic throw pillows.
9. Rachel asks the agent to add the patio set, umbrella, and pillows to cart.
   Notes: clicks not code; agent grounds answers in product information.

Section - Checkout incentive
Channel - Website
Device - MacBook
10. At checkout, Rachel receives an additional 10% checkout incentive and
    completes the purchase.

Chapter 5 — Nurture
Section - Post-purchase content
Channel - Email and iMessage
Device - iPhone
11. A post-purchase agentic message sends her hosting tips for the 4th of July
    BBQ — recipes, table styling, and party prep.
    Notes: lifecycle moment.

Chapter 6 — Service
Section - Order servicing exchange
Channel - iMessage
Device - iPhone
12. Rachel later texts At Home through iMessage to start an exchange for the
    umbrella because it arrived with a defect.
13. The AI agent identifies her order, confirms the return reason, and offers
    ship-back or in-store exchange.
    Notes: service without starting over.

Chapter 7 — Close
Section - Business value
Channel - presentation
Device - MacBook
14. Close the demo by highlighting a connected digital + physical journey
    powered by AI and customer data, leading to higher conversion rates,
    increased average order value, and lifelong loyalty.

Capabilities and platforms shown:
Agentforce AI, MCP, Data Cloud, Salesforce B2C Commerce, Marketing automation,
Personalization, Order servicing, Connected platform.

Channels and devices:
MacBook, iPhone, Instagram, Website, SMS, Email, iMessage.

Business value:
Higher conversion through timely re-engagement.
Increased average order value through AI-assisted cross-sell.
Improved loyalty and lifetime value through personalized follow-up.
Reduced service friction through automated order servicing.
A more consistent customer experience across digital and physical channels.
