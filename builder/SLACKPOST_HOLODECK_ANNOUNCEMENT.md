# Introducing Holodeck: Faster Storytelling Demos for SEs

Team — we now have **Holodeck** live for the org.

Holodeck is a demo storytelling builder that helps us create customer-specific demo experiences quickly, without heavy custom build effort. Instead of spending hours assembling one-off flows, we can move from rough notes to a credible “art of the possible” demo much earlier in the deal cycle.

## How it works (simple version)

You provide customer context (notes/script/use case), Holodeck helps generate and structure the story, and you refine it with slides, proof moments, optional app experiences (including Clienteling and Cimulate when relevant), then export for delivery. It is built for quick iteration, so as the deal evolves, your demo can evolve with it.

## Why this matters in Stage 1, 2, and 3 deals

In **Stage 1**, Holodeck helps us paint vision fast with customer-relevant storytelling instead of generic product walkthroughs.  
In **Stage 2**, it helps us iterate quickly as requirements sharpen, so the demo stays aligned with what the customer actually cares about.  
In **Stage 3**, it helps us deliver a tighter, more confident final narrative that connects proof to business value.

## Time-to-value for SEs

What used to take hours can now be much faster:
- ~**15 minutes** for a simple tailored story
- ~**1 day** for more complex, richer experiences

That means more strategic cycles per week and less last-minute demo scramble.
